## 1. What is FretGenius? (developer, platforms, launch, version, pricing; other products with the same name)

### Takeaway
FretGenius is a freemium app for learning the notes on the fretboard. It is made by a small, unfunded US company, FretGenius, LLC. It runs on iOS/iPadOS (App Store id 6745743460) and Android (package `app.fretgenius`), and parts of it run on the web (web.fretgenius.app plus free browser tools on fretgenius.app). The core on-screen drills and all lessons are free. A Pro subscription unlocks microphone training, practice on the Interval Path and the "Owlfred" Daily Coach. Pro has a 7-day trial that starts at install and cost about USD 3.99/month, or £4.99/month / £29.99/year in UK snapshots. Development is very active: iOS 2.0 shipped on 27 Aug (almost certainly 2026), and the newest version I saw was 2.8.2, from around early November 2026.

## 2. The complete inventory of exercises, modes, drills and lessons, with how each works

### Takeaway
FretGenius has about 15 training surfaces:
1. **Note Path**, a phased note-recall curriculum gated by "Phase Checks"
2. **Note Finder** (shown a note name → tap its position)
3. **Name That Note** (shown a position → name the note; timed)
4. **(Custom) Note Quiz**, with a choice of strings and frets
5. **Mic Note Trainer** (Pro): play the prompted note on a real guitar
6. **Mic Interval Trainer** (Pro)
7. **Play Along**: unscored target notes over backing chords aligned to the beat
8. **Interval Path**: lessons are free, practice is Pro
9. **Interval Finder**
10. **Scale Explorer**
11. **Chord tools**: "Find a chord", and identify a chord from a tapped shape
12. **Heatmap / Speed Map / speed charts**
13. **Daily Coach "Owlfred"** (Pro)
14. **Lessons catalog**
15. **Tuner**

It also offers free browser tools: a note trainer and a **Two-Second Challenge** speed test. Most drills are about note recall, followed by intervals. Scales and chords are offered mainly as exploration tools rather than scored drills.

### Cited Findings
**A. Note Path: the core curriculum. Lessons free; scored practice in the app.**
- **Phases:** open strings → low E string → A string → string pairs → full neck, natural notes → sharps and flats. You move on only when your accuracy and speed are good enough. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460). The method page names the last two phases "Full Fretboard" and "Full + Accidentals". [Note Path method](https://fretgenius.app/method/note-path/)
- **Gate session:** the "Phase Check" is a scored session of 35 attempts. It was called "Daily Drill" until 1.5.1. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460)
- **Advancement criteria:**
  - At least 35 recent attempts, enough accuracy, and a median time for correct answers at or below a target, currently **2 seconds**. [Note Path method](https://fretgenius.app/method/note-path/)
  - The three gates are "Accuracy + Speed + Stability". Accuracy counts correct answers, Speed is the median answer time, and Stability is a pass-streak requirement meant to stop people advancing on luck. The median was chosen because it is "harder to game than average". [Note Path method](https://fretgenius.app/method/note-path/), [fretgenius.app/fretboard-trainer](https://fretgenius.app/fretboard-trainer/)
- **Spacing:** Phase Checks are time-gated. A user complained about this, and the developer defended the spacing as helping knowledge stick long-term. [Google Play IN](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.fretgenius&hl=en_IN)
- **Restart and accidentals:**
  - The Note Path can be restarted (since 2.3.0). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460)
  - Accidentals appear only in later phases, by design. [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/fretboard-trainer-fretgenius/id6745743460)
- **Restrictions:** the learning paths support only standard-tuning guitar. [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/), [fretgenius.app/tools](https://fretgenius.app/tools/)
- **Session length:** marketed as "guided 5-minute" drills, or "about 5 minutes a day". [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius/id6745743460), [appfelstrudel](https://appfelstrudel.com/id/6745743460/fretboard-trainer-fretgenius.html)
- **Input:** the app lets you "practice with your real guitar or on-screen quizzes". [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.fretgenius&hl=en)

**B. Note Finder (free)**
- **Prompt:** a note name. **Answer:** find and tap it on the on-screen fretboard ("see a note name, find it on the guitar fretboard"). [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460), [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/)
- Time limit, scoring and string/fret range defaults were not stated in the extracts.

**C. Name That Note (free)**
- **Prompt:** a marked fret position. **Answer:** name the note. It is a **timed quiz**. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460), [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/)
- The answer interface (for example 7 or 12 note buttons) and the length of the timer were not stated.

**D. Note Quiz / "Custom Note Quiz"**
- In a review reply, the developer says you "can choose which strings and frets to practice on Tools > Custom Note Quiz". [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460), [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/fretboard-trainer-fretgenius/id6745743460)
- One listing calls the free on-screen quiz "Note Quiz (free)". [App Store IN](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460)
- The site also lists the "Note Quiz" and "Phase Checks" among the app's modes. [fretgenius.app/tools](https://fretgenius.app/tools/)

**E. Mic Note Trainer (Pro; since 1.19.0)**
- **How it works:** the app shows a target note, listens through the device microphone, and confirms when you play the correct pitch on a real guitar. [Mic trainer page](https://fretgenius.app/guitar-microphone-note-trainer/). Audio "never leaves your device". [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- **Feedback afterwards:** a session heatmap of where you were slow or inconsistent, and speed trends. [Mic trainer page](https://fretgenius.app/guitar-microphone-note-trainer/), [App Store (1.19.0)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- **Guided vs standalone:**
  - The guided mode is tuned for "Guitar + Standard E". [Mic trainer page](https://fretgenius.app/guitar-microphone-note-trainer/). The old site said "Guitar in Standard E tuning is supported today". [old site](https://fretgenius-main-site.pages.dev/)
  - The standalone mic trainers show only the note name and accept the note **in any octave**, so they also work with bass, ukulele and other string instruments. [Mic trainer page](https://fretgenius.app/guitar-microphone-note-trainer/)
- **Prompt logic and updates:**
  - Since 2.1.0 it avoids back-to-back "same note, different string" prompts. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
  - 2.8.2 improved microphone startup on iPhone. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460)

**F. Mic Interval Trainer (Pro)**
- **How it works:** a standalone mic trainer for intervals. It shows only the interval and accepts the answer in any octave. [Mic trainer page](https://fretgenius.app/guitar-microphone-note-trainer/)
- **Tier:** Pro, like the rest of interval practice. [fretgenius.app/tools](https://fretgenius.app/tools/)
- It is not stated how the root note is given.

**G. Play Along (free)**
- **How it works:** you play target notes over backing chords, with prompts landing on the beat. The aim is to use note knowledge in real playing and to train the ear in time. [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- **Input:** works on screen or with an acoustic or electric guitar. A developer reply says it lets you practice finding notes on your guitar with backing tracks. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- **Ear-training rationale:** correct notes "sound stable and strong over the music", which trains the ear to recognize consonance. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- **No scoring:** "no scoring, no pressure and no microphone required". [App Store UG](https://apps.apple.com/ug/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460). The player judges themselves, and the results do **not** count towards Note Path progress. [Note Path method](https://fretgenius.app/method/note-path/)
- **Not documented:** tempo/BPM, the choice of chord progression, key, session length, and whether the target notes are chord tones. A targeted search found nothing.

**H. Interval Path (lessons free; practice Pro; added in 2.0.3, improved in 2.7.0)**
- **Content:** teaches how two notes relate and what interval shapes look like "in every direction" on the neck. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- **Scope:** a guided curriculum from interval basics up to **triads and chords**. [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/)
- **Stages:** a screenshot shows a stage called "All Major Thirds Together", marked complete, with buttons to practice or view a lesson. ## 3. Musical content areas covered

### Takeaway
The focus is clearly on **note names** (natural notes first, then accidentals) and **intervals** (shapes, an interval path that leads to triads and chords, and mic interval practice). **Scales** appear only as an exploration and display tool, and **chords** appear as lessons and lookup tools. **Ear training** is implicit, through the consonance idea in Play Along, and **rhythm** appears only as prompts aligned to the beat. I found no evidence of CAGED, sight-reading, arpeggio drills, octave-shape drills, or a dedicated chord-tone drill.

### Cited Findings
- **Note names**, natural notes first and then sharps and flats in later phases: [Note Path method](https://fretgenius.app/method/note-path/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460)

## 6. What makes FretGenius different from typical fretboard trainers, and what it means for FretTrainer

### Takeaway
What sets FretGenius apart:
1. **Explicit, published mastery criteria:** at least 35 attempts, accuracy, a median of at most 2 s, and a pass streak, inside a string-by-string phase curriculum with spacing.
2. **Two input methods:** tapping on screen and microphone (detection on the device; any octave in the standalone modes).
3. **Unscored "Play Along"** over beat-aligned backing chords, framed as consonance ear training.
4. **An owl-mascot "AI coach"** that plans each day and schedules review.
5. **An interval-shape curriculum** that leads into triads and chords.
6. **Wide instrument and tuning customization.**
7. **A generous free tier,** with SEO guides and free browser tools pulling people in.

FretTrainer's own strengths compared with FretGenius are the scored drills with a groove and time pressure, the "closest position" rule, and scored chord-tone targeting over a progression.

### Cited Findings
- Mastery criteria and phases: [Note Path method](https://fretgenius.app/method/note-path/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-learn-guitar-notes/id6745743460)
- Tap or microphone; audio stays on the device; any octave: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.fretgenius&hl=en), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460), [Mic trainer page](https://fretgenius.app/guitar-microphone-note-trainer/)
- Play Along, unscored, with consonance as the rationale: [App Store UG](https://apps.apple.com/ug/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius-fretboard-trainer/id6745743460)
- Owlfred, the coach: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.fretgenius&hl=en)
- The Interval Path into triads and chords: [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/)
- Instruments and customization: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fretgenius/id6745743460)
- Free tier vs Pro: [fretgenius.app](https://fretgenius.app/)
- SEO guides and free tools: [Learn Center](https://fretgenius.app/learn/), [fretgenius.app/tools](https://fretgenius.app/tools/), [Speed test](https://fretgenius.app/tools/guitar-fretboard-two-second-note-challenge/)

### Inferences
These are my judgments for FretTrainer's developer, not sourced facts.
- **Ideas that would cost little to build with tapping only (no microphone):**
  1. A reverse drill ("Name That Note"): show a position and let the user choose among the 7 or 12 note names. FretTrainer's existing fretboard and audio can be reused.
  2. A short speed test (20 prompts × 2 s) that reports accuracy and **median** response time.
  3. A heatmap or speed map per position (string × fret), built from logged response times. This fits naturally into FretTrainer's existing scoring of right place / right note elsewhere / wrong.
  4. Phases with a mastery gate: E → A → string pairs → full neck → accidentals, with the gate set at, for example, a median of ≤2 s and ≥90% correct.
  5. Interval-shape drills, such as "find the major third above/below", in the spirit of "All Major Thirds Together". These could reuse FretTrainer's 6-fret positions.
  6. A free mode where the user picks strings and frets.
  7. Bringing back older phases in a daily plan (simple spaced repetition).
- **Places FretTrainer already goes beyond FretGenius:**
  - a constant groove and time pressure in scored exercises
  - the voice-leading-like rule of answering at the position closest to the previous note
  - scored chord-tone targeting (3rd/5th) over a looping progression; FretGenius's Play Along is unscored
  These are worth keeping and promoting as unique.
- **Microphone:** FretGenius charges for microphone mode and still gets detection complaints. If FretTrainer builds microphone input with the Web Audio API, start with a pitch target in any octave, which is easier, rather than an exact string and fret.

### Gaps
- Without hands-on testing or videos, I could not judge FretGenius's interface, its sound (whether there are backing tracks or a metronome in the quizzes), or how the drills feel in use.
